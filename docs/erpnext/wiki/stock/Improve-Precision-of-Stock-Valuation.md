Perform the following steps to increase the precision of your stock's valuation:

- Go to Setup > Customize Form > Select "Stock Entry Detail"

![Customize Form](http://i.imgur.com/74xBRh.png "Customize Form > Stock Entry Detail")

- Go to the field labelled "Incoming Rate" and change its field type to Float

![Incoming Rate](http://i.imgur.com/b1akoh.png "change Incoming Rate to Float")

- Click on "Update" at the top
- Go to Tools > Clear Cache & Refresh

![Test](http://i.imgur.com/eOjo8.png "Test if it works")
