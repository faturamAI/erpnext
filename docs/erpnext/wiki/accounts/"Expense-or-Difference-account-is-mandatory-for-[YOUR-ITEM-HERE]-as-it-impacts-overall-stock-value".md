### Summary of error
When saving a Delivery Note, the following error occurs:

```
"Expense or Difference account is mandatory for [YOUR ITEM HERE] as it impacts
overall stock value"
```

***
### Steps to rectify

1. Open the item detail for the item
2. Scroll to the 'Warehouse and Reference' section
3. Select an expense account for the item
