ERPNext can pull following types of emails:
* Support
* Sales
* Job

### Support Emails
* Go to Setup > Email Settings
* Setup appropriate **Incoming Email Settings**
* Check **Sync Support Mails**
* Save
* After some time, emails should appear in Support > Support Ticket

### Sales Emails
* Go to Setup > Sales Email Settings
* Setup appropriate **POP3 Email Settings**
* Check **Extract Emails**
* Save
* After some time, emails should appear in Selling > Lead as a communication thread for that Lead

### Job Applications
* Go to Setup > Jobs Email Settings
* Setup appropriate **POP3 Email Settings**
* Check **Extract Emails**
* Save
* After some time, emails should appear in HR > Job Applicant as a communication thread for that Job Applicant
